# ❄️ **ACMS**

# **Air Conditioning Management System**

> A centralized system for managing air conditioning units, maintenance, servicing, technicians, complaints, and operational records.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**ACMS (Air Conditioning Management System)** is a software system designed to efficiently manage and monitor air conditioning equipment and its related operations.

The system aims to provide a centralized platform for maintaining information about:

* 🧊 AC Units
* 🔧 Maintenance & Servicing
* 👨‍🔧 Technicians
* 📋 Service Records
* 🚨 Complaints & Issues
* 📅 Maintenance Schedules
* 💰 Maintenance Costs
* 📊 Reports & Operational Data

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Maintain complete records of AC units.
* Track servicing and maintenance history.
* Manage technician assignments.
* Record and monitor complaints.
* Schedule preventive maintenance.
* Track maintenance expenses.
* Provide organized operational information through a centralized system.
